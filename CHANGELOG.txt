Voxel Mythos v2.9.15 — "Quick Join" (faster multiplayer joins)

- Faster joins on strict networks: the relay now starts 1.5 s after a join begins (was 4 s), so friends on mobile data or school/work Wi-Fi get in a few seconds sooner. Direct play still wins whenever it opens first.
- The relay asks to join the moment its connection opens (was a fixed 0.9 s wait), and invite links start joining within 0.15 s of the title screen (was 0.9 s).
- Join progress line: "Step N of 4 · Finding the room / Connecting (direct or relay) / Downloading the world · N KB / Building the world".
- When direct play wins, the pending relay request is canceled so the host never keeps a second, orphaned link.
- Fix: the relay's last "goodbye" message is now sent when a relay room closes (it used to go nowhere).
- No world data changes; v2.9.14 and older saves load unchanged. NET_VER stays vm2; v2.9.15 and v2.9.14 players play together (direct or relay).

Voxel Mythos v2.9.14 — "Open Door" (multiplayer join fix)

- Joining works on strict networks: extra STUN plus TURN relays on 80/443 (TCP/TLS), and automatic relay play over secure public relays when a direct connection doesn't open within a few seconds (encrypted with a key from the room code, ordered and resent so nothing is lost).
- One-click invite links (?join=CODE): open the link and you land in your friend's world.
- Hosting works even when the room-code server is down; reconnects and host hand-over fall back to the relay.
- Ping and connection type (direct/relay) shown in the multiplayer bar and panel.
- Fix: a guest with no saved name no longer appears as "Title".
- No world data changes; saves from v2.9.13 and older load unchanged. NET_VER stays vm2.
