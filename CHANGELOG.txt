Voxel Mythos v2.9.16 — "Arsenal" (29 new craftable weapons, steadier multiplayer)

NEW: ARSENAL — 29 CRAFTABLE WEAPONS
- 29 new weapons to forge at a Crafting Table, each with its own sculpted model, rendered icon and stats: spears, a halberd and a glaive; bearded, long and throwing axes; mauls, war hammers, a morningstar and a flanged mace; daggers, a dirk and a stiletto; two katanas and the Starfall Odachi; a shortbow, recurves and a yew warbow; a steel arbalest; two slings; javelins; the Kraken-Tooth Saber and the Deepsworn Trident; the Cedar Pact Club; and the Forgeheart Maul.
- New ways to fight: bows draw before they loose (longer draw, faster arrow), the arbalest cranks before each shot, slings whirl, and throwing axes and javelins stick where they land so you can pick them back up (or they return to you after 45 s). Katanas have Quickdraw: the first cut after resting 1.5 s is a critical hit.
- 8 new X abilities, including Hook and Pull, Iai, Ink, Hurl, Stomp, Surge, Forgeburst and Starfall.
- The Arsenal Book (button on the Crafting Table, or /wp) lists every new weapon; Fill puts its recipe in the crafting grid.
- Rare materials come from keepsakes and finds (shed kraken teeth, Codex trophies), never from harming cryptids or sea folk.
- In multiplayer, friends on v2.9.16 see your thrown axes and javelins fly with their real models. Damage still goes through the host as before.

MULTIPLAYER
- Faster reconnects: when a connection drops, players are back in play in about 7-8 seconds (was about 14-16). A dropped link is noticed within half a second, and the redial tries direct and relay at the same time.
- Fix: when two players broke the same block or opened the same chest at the same moment, both kept the drop. Now only one does (the host decides; the other player's copy is returned).
- Smoother movement for other players: their positions are now blended between updates, so they no longer jerk when an update is lost, and they move more smoothly on slow computers.
- Fix: the relay could merge two identical messages meant for the same player into one, so one of them was lost.

SAVES AND COMPATIBILITY
- v2.9.15 and older worlds load unchanged. v2.9.16 adds one small section for your new weapons.
- A v2.9.16 world still opens in v2.9.15, but there the new weapons turn into flint knives; if you save it there, they stay flint knives.
- Same vm2 wire: v2.9.16 and v2.9.15 players can play together. Older builds don't draw the new weapon models, and the duplicate-loot fix works only when both players are on v2.9.16.

Voxel Mythos v2.9.15 — "Quick Join" (faster multiplayer joins)

- Faster joins on strict networks: the relay now starts 1.5 s after a join begins (was 4 s), so friends on mobile data or school/work Wi-Fi get in a few seconds sooner. Direct play still wins whenever it opens first.
- The relay asks to join the moment its connection opens (was a fixed 0.9 s wait), and invite links start joining within 0.15 s of the title screen (was 0.9 s).
- Join progress line: "Step N of 4 · Finding the room / Connecting (direct or relay) / Downloading the world · N KB / Building the world".
- When direct play wins, the pending relay request is canceled so the host never keeps a second, orphaned link.
- Fix: the relay's last "goodbye" message is now sent when a relay room closes (it used to go nowhere).
- No world data changes; v2.9.14 and older saves load unchanged. NET_VER stays vm2; v2.9.15 and v2.9.14 players play together (direct or relay).

Voxel Mythos v2.9.14 — "Open Door" (multiplayer join fix)

- Joining works on strict networks: extra STUN plus TURN relays on 80/443 (TCP/TLS), and automatic relay play over secure public relays when a direct connection doesn't open within a few seconds (encrypted with a key from the room code, ordered and resent so nothing is lost).
- One-click invite links (?join=CODE): open the link and you land in your friend's world.
- Hosting works even when the room-code server is down; reconnects and host hand-over fall back to the relay.
- Ping and connection type (direct/relay) shown in the multiplayer bar and panel.
- Fix: a guest with no saved name no longer appears as "Title".
- No world data changes; saves from v2.9.13 and older load unchanged. NET_VER stays vm2.
